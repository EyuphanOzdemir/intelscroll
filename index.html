For the moments you'd usually doomscroll.

Waiting for a bus. Taking a coffee break. Filling a few quiet minutes. It's easy to open a feed and keep scrolling without taking much away from it. IntelScroll gives that familiar habit a useful direction: a stream of small things to learn about something you actually care about.

Follow a passing curiosity or revisit a subject over time. Start with astronomy, Japanese history, or SQL. Make the topic as broad or specific as you like, then add instructions such as “use simple language” or “focus on practical examples.”

How does it differ from Deepstash?

Deepstash helps people discover short ideas from books, articles, podcasts and videos, and offers both free and Pro plans. IntelScroll takes a different approach: you define the topic and an AI model generates cards for it on demand. Its focus is giving you control over what you explore and how you learn.

Your topics, your instructions.

Create your own subjects and guide the explanations with a custom prompt. Explore a niche interest or a specific skill without waiting for it to appear in a content library.

New cards when you ask.

Each new card is generated for your selected topic and mode, rather than selected from a prewritten catalogue. Continue exploring while your provider's usage limits allow, or tap Expand for more detail on the current card.

Choose the AI behind your feed.

Use the Gemini starter with an invitation, bring your own provider key, or connect a custom provider. Select the model you prefer for your topic and budget.

Keep what you learn.

Save cards to your local collection and revisit them by topic. Share an entry, or export a topic's saved entries as a text file to keep in Downloads or use elsewhere.

Get started.

  1. Download this APK on your Android phone.
  2. Open it. If Android asks, allow installation from your browser or file manager, then tap Install.
  3. On first launch, enter an invitation code for the Gemini starter, or choose I don't have one and add your own AI provider on Models.
  4. Add a subject on Topics. On Home, choose your topic, model and learning mode, then tap New or swipe right.

Once installed, the app runs without a computer. To update, install the new APK over your existing IntelScroll app; your saved entries and settings are retained.

Choose your provider. Unlock Gemini with an invitation.

An invitation code unlocks Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite during first-launch setup. Email ozdemireyuphan@gmail.com to request a code. Availability depends on quotas and provider limits.

Choosing I don't have one permanently dismisses invitation setup and hides the starter. You can then add your own model on the Models tab. Existing users see this setup once after updating; their custom models and saved entries are retained.

Use your own API key from Gemini, OpenAI, DeepSeek, Kimi, Grok, OpenRouter or Claude, or connect a custom provider.

Your own API keys are stored securely on your device; saved entries stay local. Prompts are sent to your chosen provider. Generation needs network access, and usage charges may apply when using your own provider.

Cards are AI-generated and can contain mistakes. Use them as a starting point for learning, and check facts that matter to you against reliable sources.
